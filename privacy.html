Privacy

Exactly what leaves your Mac, when, and why. Short version: your files never leave your Mac.

Always on your Mac

Scanning, measuring, the traffic-light verdicts, the Trash flow and the restore manifests run entirely locally. The app never uploads your files, your folder listings or your document names.

The optional AI analysis

Only when you tap the ✦ button on an item, the app sends three small things to the AI provider: the item's name, its path, and the verdict already decided by our local rules. It never sends file contents, never a list of your documents, never anything you didn't explicitly ask to explain. The reply includes the web sources cited by the model.

With web search enabled (opt-in, one search costs 7 of the included AI requests), the model also consults public pages — Apple support documents, community answers — to explain unfamiliar items. The engine sees a question about a public folder name; it never sees your data.

Zero telemetry

No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no profiles. The only thing our license server knows about you is a number: how many AI requests of your license remain, so the app can show "397 of 400 remaining". Purchases are processed by Gumroad (payment data lives there, we never see your card).

Knowledge base updates

The knowledge base runs offline. Updates happen only when you click "Check for updates" in the app's settings, and each update is an Ed25519-signed file the app verifies before installing.

What we'd like to be crystal clear about

The app asks for Full Disk Access to measure the real size of system data — that's read-only measurement, and the app works even without it. We chose a deterministic rule engine plus an AI that only explains (never decides) precisely so that the powerful part of the app never depends on the cloud.
